# 📊 Power BI Dashboard Creation — Map of Content

This is the master playbook for building a Power BI dashboard from scratch — from the first client conversation to the final published report.

Follow the phases in order. Each phase has its own detailed note.

---

## The 7 Phases

| # | Phase | What it covers |
|---|-------|----------------|
| 1 | [[03_powerbi/01_client_discovery\|🧭 Client Discovery]] | Questions to ask, what the client has, what they want |
| 2 | [[03_powerbi/02_data_sources\|🗄️ Data Sources]] | Where data comes from, formats, ingestion, cleaning |
| 3 | [[03_powerbi/03_data_model\|🧩 Data Model]] | Tables, schema design, primary keys, relationships |
| 4 | [[03_powerbi/04_dax_calculations\|🔢 DAX & Calculations]] | Measures, calculated columns, new tables, calendar tables |
| 5 | [[03_powerbi/05_kpis_and_metrics\|🎯 KPIs & Metrics]] | What to show, business questions to answer |
| 6 | [[03_powerbi/06_design_and_layout\|🎨 Design & Layout]] | Visual selection, layout grid, colors, sizes, interactivity |
| 7 | [[03_powerbi/07_build_and_interactivity\|⚙️ Build & Interactivity]] | Creating visuals, buttons, bookmarks, page navigation |

---

## How to use this

1. Start at Phase 1 every time — even if you think you know what the client wants
2. Each phase note has a **checklist** you can copy per project
3. Decisions made in earlier phases affect all later ones — don't skip
4. Come back and update these notes as you learn new patterns
