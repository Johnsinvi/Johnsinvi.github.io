# 🧭 Phase 1 — Client Discovery

> **Goal:** Understand the business context, who will use the dashboard, what decisions it needs to support, and what data already exists.

← Back to [[03_powerbi/Power BI - Map of Content]]
Next → [[03_powerbi/02_data_sources]]

---

## Why this phase matters

Skipping discovery is the #1 reason dashboards get rebuilt. A dashboard built on assumptions will look good but answer the wrong questions. Always validate before you model.

---

## Questions to ask the client

### About the business
- What is the main problem you want this dashboard to solve?
- What decisions will people make using this dashboard?
- What does success look like — what would a "good" dashboard let you do that you can't do today?

### About the audience
- Who will use this dashboard? (executives, managers, analysts, operations?)
- How technical is the audience — do they understand filters, drill-downs, slicers?
- How often will they look at it — daily, weekly, monthly?
- Will they use it on desktop, mobile, or both?

### About existing reports
- Do you have any existing reports, Excel files, or dashboards we should reference?
- Are there specific metrics or KPIs you already track manually?
- Is there a specific layout or visual style you like or want to avoid?

### About the data
- What systems hold your data? (ERP, CRM, Excel files, database, cloud service?)
- Do you have access to those systems, or will data be exported for us?
- How often does the data update — real-time, daily, weekly?
- Is there a person or team responsible for the data?

### About constraints
- Are there any fields or data that should NOT appear in the dashboard (sensitive, confidential)?
- Is there a deadline or a specific date the dashboard needs to be ready?
- Will this be embedded in another platform, or used directly in Power BI Service?

---

## Checklist

- [ ] Identified the main business question the dashboard answers
- [ ] Confirmed who the end users are and their technical level
- [ ] Understood the update frequency needed
- [ ] Listed all data sources mentioned by the client
- [ ] Noted existing reports or references to replicate
- [ ] Identified any access restrictions or sensitive fields
- [ ] Agreed on a delivery timeline

---

## Output of this phase

A short written summary (even just your notes) that captures:
- The dashboard's purpose in one sentence
- The audience and how they'll use it
- The data sources to connect
- Any constraints or requirements

This becomes your reference document for every decision in the phases that follow.
